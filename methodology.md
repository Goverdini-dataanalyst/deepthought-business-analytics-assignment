# DeepThought Assignment Methodology

## City Chosen
Chennai

## Segments Chosen
- Medical Devices
- Electronics & Defence Manufacturing

## Research Process
I researched specialty manufacturing companies in Chennai using:
- Company websites
- LinkedIn
- Industry directories
- Google search
- News and expansion announcements

## Qualification Process
Each company was evaluated using DeepThought’s 6 Federer criteria:
1. Manufacturer
2. India-based
3. Differentiated products
4. Technical decision-maker
5. Growing sector
6. Growth signals

## Growth Signals Checked
- Hiring activity
- Expansion announcements
- Product launches
- Export activity
- Partnerships

## Tools Used
- Excel
- Google Search
- LinkedIn
- ChatGPT for structuring and formatting
